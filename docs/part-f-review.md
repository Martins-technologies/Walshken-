# Part F: attendance, shifts, breaks and timesheets

Build on Part E without changing production. Extend the canonical attendance, breaks, shifts, timesheets, corrections, leave_requests and holidays tables. Tag Preview data separately and enforce production isolation through restrictive RLS. Add employee break policies, recurring-series metadata, shift-swap requests, absence records and immutable workforce events. No sample employees, shifts or attendance are deployed.

Clock events are independent from authentication/session logs and task timers. Server timestamps drive real attendance; manual entries require explicit per-employee authorization or scoped management permission and always require independent review. All mutations serialize through the organization row, recheck current scope, use revisions, reject overlapping work/breaks and retain correction history. Approved corrections atomically replace working intervals and break versions, recalculate payable time and resubmit timesheets. No employee or manager can approve their own hours. Only scoped admins/HR/Super Admin can approve corrections. They may propose a correction on behalf of a suspended or ended employee, but a different authorized administrator must review that proposal.

Alberta standard adult rules are the initial supported jurisdiction, confirmed by the owner. Source: https://www.alberta.ca/hours-work-rest (checked 2026-11-23). Do not infer statutory exemptions from portal roles. Empty/unsupported employment jurisdictions must be configured before schedules or final approvals. Policies cannot reduce the statutory break minimum, twelve-hour standard shift span, eight-hour rest between shifts or the twenty-four-hour change notice. This initial standard policy retains uninterrupted statutory 30-minute rests; additional paid 15-minute breaks are supported. Restricted breaks must be paid. Actual missed/late breaks remain visible compliance incidents; approving actual payable time requires documented review and never erases the incident or invents an unpaid deduction. No claims about overtime, holiday-pay amounts or other countries' law; these belong to payroll/legal configuration.

Shifts support draft/published/cancelled states, multiple assignees per occurrence, finite weekly recurrence, explicit employee-local zones, daylight-saving-safe local-time conversion, overlap/rest/leave checks, future editing, publication notifications and consent plus scoped approval for swaps. Weekly/monthly calendars show published work and approved leave/holidays. Attendance records scheduled versus unscheduled work, late starts, early departures and no-shows. A Preview-only SQL maintenance job records absences and alerts on overdue clock-outs without fabricating clock-out times.

Employee/manager dashboards show running work and breaks, interval totals, correction queues, timesheet submissions/reviews and daily/weekly/monthly report exports. Reports split overnight intervals at local midnight; payroll-ready approved seconds remain separate from recorded seconds. Authoritative server checks protect every action and CSV export. API responses never contain salary or unrelated employee records.

Acceptance: automated route tests for role/MFA/scope/revocation, all clock/break transitions, 8h minus 30m equals 7.5h, paid/restricted/custom/multiple breaks, statutory minimums, recurrence/DST/overlap, swaps, leave/holidays/absences, corrections and independent review, overnight reports, stale updates, session separation and Preview isolation. Preserve existing passwords and MFA. Owner manually tests the combined E/F Preview before production release.

Alberta permanent UTC−6 from November 2026 is handled explicitly in application and Preview maintenance calculations, including runtimes with older IANA data. Source: https://www.alberta.ca/albertas-new-time-system-abt. DST gaps/folds and recurrence are also tested with America/New_York, which retains seasonal changes.
